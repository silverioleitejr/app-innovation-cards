## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a clean corporate editorial aesthetic tailored for high-velocity teams, strategic planners, and modern enterprise leadership. It combines the rigorous precision of an elite publication with the kinetic clarity of a real-time productivity workspace.

### Core Tenets
- **Editorial Authority:** Pure black titles set in gossamer, ultra-light sans-serif weights evoke print refinement and intellectual clarity.
- **Electric Precision:** Interactive surfaces snap into focus through the signature OG1 Blue, creating unambiguous affordance amidst stark white planes.
- **Tonal Warmth:** A foundation of calibrated warm grays tempers structural tension, ensuring extended sessions remain visually hospitable without sacrificing corporate rigor.
- **Dual-Header Architecture:** Hierarchy is strictly delineated through stacked global orchestration and contextual module navigation.

## Colors

The palette operates on stark contrast balanced by soft architectural neutrals.

- **Primary (`#0b41cd`):** "OG1 Blue." Reserved strictly for focused interactions, primary actions, active navigational states, and focus rings.
- **Neutral Base:** Pure `#ffffff` defines primary canvases. `#dbd6d1` acts as hairline borders and structural dividers, while `#9d948e` and `#544f4f` handle secondary metadata and subdued copy.
- **Headings & Text:** Pure black (`#000000`) for headers down to medium labels; body text rests at `#1a1a1a` to preserve contrast.
- **Alert / Error (`#ff1f26`):** Critical status indicators, destructive confirmations, and input validation warnings.
- **Atmospheric Gradient:** Hero surfaces utilize a linear top-down or diagonal shift from a warm linen tint (`#fbf9f7`) softening into a cool, luminous azure mist (`#f0f4ff`).

## Typography

The system uses **Outfit** for editorial display and headline hierarchies, pairing its geometric, open proportions at ultra-light weights (`200` and `300`) with pure `#000000` ink. This deliberate tension produces a razor-sharp, editorial tone.

For sustained reading and data consumption, **Inter** serves as the utilitarian workhorse. It delivers optical clarity across high-density agile boards, tables, form components, and navigation structures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an adaptive 12-column grid anchored by a dual-tier navigation system:
1. **Global Header:** Fixed 56px height, containing workspace switching, system-level search, and global actions.
2. **Module Sub-Header:** 48px height, containing project filters, sprint views, view switchers, and localized deck controls.

### Breakpoints & Flow
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 32px margins. Modules display parallel columns (e.g., Kanban boards, split preview panels).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Toolbars compress secondary actions into unified overflow menus.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 12px gutters, 16px margins. Module headers switch from inline tabs to horizontally scrollable rails.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes surface boundaries over excessive shadow weight:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** The primary separation method uses a 1px solid `#dbd6d1` border.
- **Subtle Drop Shadows:** Reserved for elevated states, dropdown menus, flyouts, and floating sprint toolbars. Shadows are soft and diffused: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(84, 79, 79, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(84, 79, 79, 0.04)`.
- **Card States:** Resting cards sit flat with a 1px border. On drag or hover, elements translate `-2px` along the Y-axis and acquire an intensified warm-gray shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(84, 79, 79, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an intentional **6px standard border radius** across all functional core primitives (inputs, buttons, chips, standard cards, and modals). This specific micro-radius produces a crisp, architectural silhouette that avoids clinical sharpness while resisting playful over-rounding.

- Standard modules, buttons, and form inputs: `6px` (`rounded-sm`).
- Outer application panels and dialog windows: `8px` (`rounded-md`).
- Fully rounded pills are reserved exclusively for counter badges and live status dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0b41cd` background, white text, 6px border radius, no border. Subtle brightness shift on hover (`#0935a8`).
- **Secondary:** White background, 1px solid `#dbd6d1` border, `#1a1a1a` text. Hover shifts border color to `#9d948e`.
- **Ghost / Link:** Transparent background, `#0b41cd` text, hover reveals a faint `#f0f4ff` tint.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs:** 1px solid `#dbd6d1` container, 6px radius, pure white background. Focus applies an electric 1px border in `#0b41cd` along with a 2px outer ring of `rgba(11, 65, 205, 0.15)`.
- **Error State:** Border becomes `#ff1f26`, paired with helper text in `#ff1f26`.

### Cards & Deck Modules
- **Deck Card:** `#ffffff` surface, 1px solid `#dbd6d1` border, 6px radius. Card header features light-weight Outfit typography with secondary metadata in `#544f4f`.
- **Interactive Board Columns:** Transparent or soft linen-tinted backdrop with dashed `#dbd6d1` drop zones.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicators:** Neutral states use `#fbf9f7` filled containers with `#544f4f` text.
- **Active / Assigned:** Bordered in `#0b41cd` with `#0b41cd` typography at 500 weight.
- **Bug / Alert:** Tinted `#fff1f1` background with `#ff1f26` text and border.

### Headers
- **Global Header:** Pure white background, bottom 1px border `#dbd6d1`. Houses brandmark and global navigation.
- **Module Header:** Sits immediately below the global header, backed by a subtle `#faf8f6` to `#f4f6fc` micro-gradient or crisp white, separated by a 1px horizontal rule in `#dbd6d1`.
